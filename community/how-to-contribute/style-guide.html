HexOS Documentation Style Guide

Tone / language

  • Friendly
  • Clear
  • Avoid things that are difficult to translate or for non-native english speakers such as:
    • MetaphorcTurns of phrase
    • Pop culture references

Capitalisation

Sentence case is used for heading, so the first letter of the first word is capitalised and otherwise only propper nouns are capitalised.

Accessibility

  • Rename screenshot before adding to github, this automatically puts alt-text as something human readable (for screenreader)

Referring to UI

  • Direct quote from app e.g button names should follow the capitalisation of the app
  • Direct quotes from app should be in bold
    • E.g. Press the Continue button
  • Menu navigation format Item > Item

Terminology

  • click v tap ?
  • screen v page?

Image styles

  • Dark mode or light?
  • What to include what to crop
  • What to pixelise

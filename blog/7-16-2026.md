# Buddy Backups, New App Curations, and What's Next for HexOS

It's been a busy few months since HexOS 1.0 arrived, and we wanted to take a moment to give you a clear picture of where things stand across our biggest initiatives — Buddy Backups, app curations, preconfiguration, and Virtual Machines.

## Buddy Backups: The Final Stretch

Buddy Backups has been our top priority since launching 1.0, and we're happy to report the project is nearing completion and expect to begin rolling this out for testing very soon.

For anyone new to the concept: Buddy Backups will give you an easy way to back up your data to a friend or family member's HexOS server that you trust — and vice versa. No complicated networking setup, no third-party cloud storage bill. Just secure, peer-to-peer backups between people you know.

We'll be sharing a dedicated blog post soon with a deeper look at how Buddy Backups works under the hood. In the meantime, here's the timeline:

- **Early Access launch:** coming soon
- **Stabilization:** targeted for Q4

We're intentionally launching in an early access capacity first so we can gather real-world feedback before the feature is considered fully stable.

## Apps: 16 New Curations Since 1.0

Our apps team hasn't slowed down. Since HexOS 1.0 launched in April, we've added **16 new curated apps** to the catalog, with plenty more in the pipeline. Some recent additions include tools for media management, note-taking, log viewing, and self-hosted bookmarking — with new curations shipping on a near-weekly cadence.

If you want the full rundown of what's been added and when, check out our [Command Deck release notes](https://docs.hexos.com/release-notes/command-deck/), where every new curation is announced as it ships.

## Preconfiguration: Making Setup Even Easier

Alongside new curations, preconfiguration remains a major focus for the apps team. The goal is simple: reduce the number of decisions and manual steps standing between you and actually using the apps you care about.

We'll continue rolling out preconfiguration support for additional apps in the near future, so keep an eye on the release notes for updates as they land.

## Up Next: Virtual Machines

Once Buddy Backups ships and stabilizes, the team's focus shifts to the last major feature we promised at launch: **Virtual Machines**.

As with everything else on our roadmap, the goal is to cut down on the complexity users typically face when setting up and configuring VMs — making advanced use cases feel a lot less advanced to get running.

---

That's the state of the roadmap as of today. We'll keep these updates coming as Buddy Backups gets closer to early access and we have more to share on both the apps and VM fronts. Thanks for being part of the journey.

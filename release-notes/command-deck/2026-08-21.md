# August 21, 2026 - New app curations (Ratelog & Swiparr)

## New curated apps

- [Ratelog](https://ratelog.org/en/) — Ratelog is an app for rating movies and TV shows with a detailed category-based scoring system.
- [Swiparr](https://github.com/m3sserstudi0s/swiparr) — Swiparr turns the dreaded "what should we watch?" question into a fun, collaborative experience.

### Curation notes

- **Swiparr**
  - The default media server URLs are already filled in.
  - Log in using the same credentials you would use for Jellyfin or Emby.
  - The first account that logs in becomes the admin account for Swiparr.
  - We recommend enabling guest landing in the settings to make it easier for your friends and family to join a group without needing to log in.

> **Thank you:** Jakob Bjelvér for developing Swiparr and submitting it to the TrueNAS catalog at our request.

# November 3, 2026 - Restore points, Recently deleted folders, multiple app copies, and more

Here's what shipped since our [last update](/release-notes/command-deck/2026-10-30).

## Restore points

- **Restore points for folders, apps and VMs.** Open the **Restore points** tab to click **Take now** or set a **Schedule**. Restore in place with 7 days to undo, or restore a folder or VM as a copy beside the original.
- **Get back a single file.** **Browse files** opens a restore point so you can bring back only what you need.
- New folders get a schedule automatically. Existing folders, apps and VMs need one turned on from their **Restore points** tab.

## Folders

- **Recently deleted.** Deleting a folder now moves it to **Recently deleted** for 30 days, with its files, access and restore points. Bring it back with **Restore folder**, or choose **Delete forever** to free the space right away.
- **Find folders.** The **Folders** page now has a search box.

## Apps

- **Run more than one copy of an app.** Host a second Valheim world, another Palworld server or several Minecraft servers. Click **Install another** on the app's page, and each copy gets its own name, folders and ports.
- **Port conflicts are handled for you.** If a port is taken, HexOS moves the app to a free one, or tells you what's using a port the app can't do without.
- **Jellyfin hardware transcoding.** HexOS turns it on when your server's graphics can handle it, and adds the firmware low-power Intel chips like the Celeron N5105 need.
- Plex, Jellyfin and Emby cards show sharper artwork.
- In some cases, app updates changed permissions on folders other apps share, Jellyfin added the same library more than once, or **Connect to Jellystat** failed with Jellyfin 12. These are fixed.

## Buddy Backups

- **Your buddy keeps your history.** If restore points are deleted or rolled back on your server, by mistake or by ransomware, your buddy's server keeps its copies of your recent ones.
- **Keep an older backup and start fresh.** When your backup and your server no longer match, keep the existing backup and start a new one beside it.
- Transfer speeds are measured more accurately, and servers on the same network back up at full local speed.

## Power

- **Manage existing UPS in HexOS** takes over automatic shutdown for a UPS you set up in TrueNAS. **Manage only in TrueNAS** hands it back.
- **Replace primary UPS** switches to a new UPS, with **Restore previous UPS** if anything goes wrong.
- **A/B shutdown protection** for servers with two power supplies, each on its own UPS.
- More USB UPS models work, and the **Power** screen now shows your main UPS first.

## Virtual machines

- New Plex VMs reach your media folders directly instead of through a network share.
- In some cases, the VM console opened blank, or a removed VM's disks couldn't be deleted. These are fixed.

## Everything else

- Page actions like **New pool**, **New folder** and **Update all** now sit at the top of each page, next to search.
- Your browser picks up new Command Deck versions right away, instead of hours later.
- In some cases on TrueNAS 26, a server showed offline after a restart, or setting up a VM or sharing a folder failed with a permissions error. These are fixed.

**NOTE:** These updates apply automatically. You may need to [clear your cache](/troubleshooting/browser-cache).

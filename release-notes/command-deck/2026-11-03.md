# November 3, 2026 - Buddy Backups protect your history, hardware transcoding for Jellyfin, more ways to set up your UPS, and more

Here's what shipped since our [last update](/release-notes/command-deck/2026-10-30).

## Buddy Backups

- **Your buddy keeps your history.** If restore points disappear from your server, whether someone deletes them, a folder is rolled back too far, or ransomware wipes them, your buddy's server now keeps its copies of your recent restore points instead of deleting them on the next backup. Your folder's backup shows when your buddy has confirmed the points are protected.
- **Keep an older backup and start fresh.** When your backup and your server no longer match, you can now keep the existing backup as it is and start a new one beside it, with space your buddy approves. You can restore from either copy.
- In some cases, the **Transfer speed** card showed a speed far above what your connection can carry, which also stopped **Balanced** and **Background** speeds from limiting anything. Speeds are now measured from what each backup actually sent.
- In some cases, a first backup that was interrupted kept pausing for space even though it would fit. It now picks up where it left off.

## Media apps

- **Jellyfin uses your graphics for transcoding.** When Jellyfin can use your server's graphics to convert video, HexOS now turns hardware transcoding on for you, so playback keeps up instead of stalling. New installs get it automatically. On an existing install, click **Set up transcoding and remote streaming** on the Jellyfin card.
- **Smoother playback on small servers.** Low-power Intel chips, like the Celeron N5105 in many compact NAS boxes, need extra firmware for hardware video. HexOS adds it for you, and Jellyfin asks you to restart when it suits you. HexOS never restarts your server on its own.
- **Sharper card artwork.** Plex, Jellyfin and Emby cards now show larger, clearer pictures of what's playing.
- In some cases, Jellyfin's setup added the same library several times on a busy server. It now checks what's already there first.
- In some cases, **Connect to Jellystat** couldn't sign in to Jellyfin 12. It connects again.
- In some cases, the Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and Tautulli cards stayed on their setup message, or showed no picture, while the app was working. They now show your library and what's playing.

## Power

- **Use a UPS that's already set up in TrueNAS.** If you set up your UPS in TrueNAS, click **Manage existing UPS in HexOS** to control its automatic shutdown from Command Deck. Your TrueNAS settings stay as they are, and **Manage only in TrueNAS** hands control back anytime.
- **Replace primary UPS.** Switch to a new UPS, even when the old one is disconnected. HexOS tests the new connection first, and you can click **Restore previous UPS** if anything doesn't work out.
- **More USB UPS models work.** Some UPSes don't report a unique serial number. You can now pick these by the USB port they're plugged into.
- **Protection for servers with two power supplies.** If each of your server's power supplies has its own UPS, **A/B shutdown protection** keeps your server running while either UPS has power, and shuts it down safely only when neither does. Each side can be a USB or network UPS.
- **A simpler Power screen.** Your main UPS readings and shutdown settings now come first. Extra devices, replacement and USB details are under **Manage UPS devices**.
- In some cases, starting USB monitoring for a UPS that TrueNAS already watches showed a general error. Command Deck now explains that TrueNAS is using that device and points you to its readings.
- A USB UPS can now be monitored alongside a network UPS that TrueNAS manages.

## A tidier layout

- **Actions where you expect them.** **New pool**, **New folder**, **Update all**, **Install from script** and **Create custom VM** now sit at the top of their pages, next to search, instead of at the end of the card grid.
- **Find folders.** The **Folders** page now has a search box.

## Apps

- In some cases, updating an app changed the permissions on a folder that several apps share, so another app lost access. App updates now leave shared folders' permissions as they are.
- A stopped app's card no longer opens wide just to say the app isn't running.

## Virtual machines

- **The Plex VM reads your media folders directly.** New Plex VMs now reach your media folders straight from your server instead of through a network share, so there's no share password stored in the VM.
- In some cases, the VM console opened to a blank or unstyled page. It loads correctly again.
- In some cases on TrueNAS 26, setting up a VM, sharing a folder or moving one failed with a permissions error. These work again.

## Everything else

- **Updates arrive sooner.** Your browser now picks up a new Command Deck version right after it ships, instead of up to a few hours later.
- In some cases, the page asking you to switch to a secure (HTTPS) address appeared without its styling. It displays correctly again.

**NOTE:** These updates apply automatically. You may need to [clear your cache](/troubleshooting/browser-cache).

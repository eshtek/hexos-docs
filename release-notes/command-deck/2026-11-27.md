# November 27, 2026 - Phone and TV apps with HexOS Anywhere, app startup order, easier VM setup, and more

Here's what shipped since our [last update](/release-notes/command-deck/2026-11-24).

## HexOS Anywhere

- **More phone and TV apps wait for your approval.** When you pointed a phone or TV app at an Anywhere address with sign-in on, many apps would stop at their first request, and nothing showed up for you to approve. The only way through was to turn sign-in off for the app. The Nextcloud apps, SparkyFitness, Actuali for Actual Budget and Conduit for Open WebUI now wait in your device list until you approve them.
- **Name your devices.** Click **Rename** on an approved device to give it a name and an icon, so you can tell your devices apart. Apps that can send a device name show up under it, like "Conduit on John's iPhone".
- Approving a device now also lifts any lockout its earlier sign-in attempts caused.

## Apps

- **Choose which apps start with your server, and in what order.** When your server restarted, every app that had been running would come back all at once. A VPN app came up alongside the apps that route through it, and a database came up alongside the app that needs it. Click **Startup Order** on the **Installed apps** page to put your apps in order. Each app waits for the one before it to start. Apps you move under **Start by hand** stay stopped until you start them. The same switch is on each app's **Customize** tab.
- In some cases, when you switched between servers quickly, a page could show or act on the server you just left. Pages across the Command Deck now stay with the server you picked, from apps and storage to VMs, Buddy Backups and settings.
- When a page couldn't load part of its information, it would sometimes stay empty with no way forward. These pages now say what couldn't be read and offer **Retry**.

## Virtual machines

- **Set exact amounts for memory and cores.** On a big server, the memory and core sliders were hard to land on an exact amount. You can now type an amount, and the sliders snap to common sizes.
- **Delete a broken ISO.** When a downloaded ISO came out corrupted, there was no clear way to delete it. You can now delete an ISO right from the VM media picker, and a failed install offers to delete the ISO it used.
- **Graphics cards bring their companions along.** In some cases, when you picked a graphics card for a VM, its onboard USB controller was left behind, and the VM couldn't start. The card's audio, USB and USB-C controllers now come with it, and devices plugged into the card's own USB ports are marked **On card**.
- In some cases, a VM wouldn't start after one of its USB devices was unplugged, and the device no longer showed so you could remove it. The missing device now shows on the VM, so you can take it off and start the VM.
- In some cases, CachyOS one-click installs stopped at the live session with nothing installed. They now finish.
- In some cases, Fedora KDE VMs opened KDE's first-run setup wizard after installing. They now go straight to the sign-in screen.

## Bring over

- **Choose which folder copies next.** When several folders are waiting, click **Copy next** on one to move it to the front of the line.
- In some cases, Bring over could copy files into a new folder that an installed app already expected for its own data. The app would then fail to start. Bring over no longer reuses a folder an installed app depends on.

## Buddy Backups and power

- When your browser went offline during a transfer, the last measured speed would stay on screen as if it were current. It now hides until your browser reconnects.
- Space requests for a backup you already removed no longer show up.
- In some cases, after you replaced a USB UPS, **Power** settings kept showing the disconnected one. You can now remove it.

## Licences

- When you're signed in to your server's own Command Deck with TrueNAS, **License required** now offers **Open Command Deck** so you can pick a plan for the server. The license messages now name your server.
- You can now pay for a plan with PayPal at [hexos.com](https://hexos.com).

## Everything else

- In some cases, when a reverse proxy answered in place of the Command Deck, the offline page showed placeholder text instead of its recovery steps. It now shows the steps.
- We tightened security checks across HexOS Anywhere, VM networking and the connection between your server and HexOS Cloud.

**NOTE:** These updates apply automatically. You may need to [clear your cache](/troubleshooting/browser-cache).

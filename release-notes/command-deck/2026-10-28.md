# October 28, 2026 - Bring over for everyone, UPS monitoring, nine new apps, and more

Here's what shipped since our [last update](/release-notes/command-deck/2026-10-16).

## Bring over

- **Now available to everyone.** Copy files from another computer, an external drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud Drive or iCloud Photos. Google Drive is coming once Google finishes its review.
- **Clearer progress.** Each share shows a progress bar and how much has been copied.
- **Pause and resume** picks up where you left off.

## Power

- **UPS monitoring.** Plug in a UPS over USB and a **Power** tile shows its charge, load and time on battery, and warns you when the battery needs attention.
- HexOS turns on monitoring for you. To have your server shut down when the battery runs low, turn it on in **Settings** > **Power**.

## A new welcome

- New servers get a short welcome that offers **Hardware advisor** and a memory test, then shortcuts to get started. Reopen it from **Settings** > **First-time setup**.
- Setup is shorter, and the storage choices are now **Let HexOS decide**, **I'll configure myself** and **Use imported pools only**.

## Storage

- **Unlock passphrase-protected pools** right from the pool page after a restart. If the pool holds your apps, the dashboard offers **Unlock** first.
- Drive tests that erase a drive are now offered only for unused drives.
- NVMe drives that can't self-test no longer fail pool audits over old, unchanged error counts.
- Moving your apps to another pool now reports its progress correctly.

## Apps

- Updating several apps at once is more reliable, and downloads retry longer when Docker Hub is busy.
- Apps that need a display, like Kodi, explain when your server has no graphics card for them.
- Fixed cases where an update showed as failed while the app was still starting, or where an app with its own database (like Jellystat) couldn't start after an update.

## Virtual machines

- **The Plex VM now keeps Plex up to date**, including existing installs.
- VMs now show the right time, and older Windows 11 installers like 23H2 now install.
- The VM console opens through your own domain without **Not Authenticated** errors.
- **Set up a VM** no longer shows errors before you type anything.

## Buddy Backups

- **Change server** now explains why a server can't take your backup.
- The backups tables fit on phones and smaller laptops.
- An offline buddy now shows a clear "couldn't reach" message.

## Everything else

- The dashboard loads noticeably faster.
- Your chosen language stays put as you move around.
- Changing your password signs you out everywhere else.
- Key details for **Repair access**, backup requests and VM removal are now on the screen, not hidden in tooltips.

## New curated apps

- [Kodi](https://kodi.tv) - Plays your movies, shows and music on a TV plugged into your server. Our first display app.
- [Tautulli](https://tautulli.com) - Watch history and stats for Plex, with a dashboard card.
- [SABnzbd](https://sabnzbd.org) and [NZBGet](https://nzbget.com) - Usenet downloaders.
- [Deluge](https://deluge-torrent.org) - A lightweight BitTorrent client.
- [Profilarr](https://github.com/Dictionarry-Hub/profilarr) - Quality profiles for Radarr and Sonarr.
- [Vaultwarden](https://github.com/dani-garcia/vaultwarden) - A Bitwarden-compatible password manager. Leave the admin panel password blank to keep the panel off.
- [Uptime Kuma](https://uptime.kuma.pet) - Keep an eye on your websites and services, and get alerted when one goes down.
- [Crafty 4](https://craftycontrol.com) - Run and manage your own Minecraft servers.

## Media apps that work together

- **Connect apps in one click.** Radarr, Sonarr, Lidarr, Prowlarr, Seerr, Bazarr, Profilarr, your download clients and your media server can now link to each other, from the install dialog or an app's **Connections** tab.
- **One admin account.** Choose a single username and password at install instead of default sign-ins.
- **Ready on first open.** Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr start with your Movies, Shows and Music folders already added.
- Setting up Jellyfin, Emby and Plex is optional and can be run later from the app's card.
- Emby's card shows what's playing, and Scrutiny's card adds disk temperatures.

**NOTE:** These updates apply automatically. You may need to [clear your cache](/troubleshooting/browser-cache).

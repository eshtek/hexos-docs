# November 17, 2026 - A faster Command Deck at home, Stardew Valley and Rust servers, and more

Here's what shipped since our [last update](/release-notes/command-deck/2026-11-13).

## A faster Command Deck at home

When you open [deck.hexos.com](https://deck.hexos.com) on the same network as your server, the Command Deck now talks to your server directly. Pages like Storage and Folders load in a fraction of the time.

- **No more jump to a second page.** Before, the Command Deck moved you to your server's own deck a moment after it opened, and asked you to sign in again. Now you stay where you are and sign in once.
- **Look for the purple Direct badge.** While the Command Deck is talking to your server directly, a purple **Direct** badge shows beside your server's name. The blue **Local** badge still means you're on your server's own deck.
- **It falls back by itself.** If your server stops answering on your network, the Command Deck goes back through HexOS Cloud in a few seconds. The page keeps working, with no error.
- The **You are on the hosted Command Deck** strip across the top of every page is gone. The server menu still tells you which way you're connected, and **Open local deck** still takes you to your server's own deck.

## HexOS Anywhere

- **More apps work at their Anywhere address.** In some cases, Nextcloud answered its Anywhere address with **Access through untrusted domain**. SparkyFitness and Paperless-ngx refused the sign-in, and Firefly III loaded without its styles. HexOS now tells these apps about their Anywhere address, and tells them again whenever it changes.
- **Bitwarden phone apps work with Vaultwarden.** In some cases, the Bitwarden apps said a Vaultwarden Anywhere address was not a recognized Bitwarden server. They now show up in your device list for you to approve.
- **Phone apps no longer get blocked by mistake.** In some cases, when sign-in was turned off for an app's address, apps like Swiftfin were locked out a few seconds after signing in to Jellyfin. Only real password guesses now count toward the lockout.
- **Addresses stuck on Connecting.** In some cases, on servers that first installed HexOS a while ago, an Anywhere address stayed on **Connecting** for hours or days. HexOS now repairs the setting it needs and connects the server.
- **Put Anywhere back on a server.** If you took HexOS Anywhere off a server, your [account page](https://hexos.com/account) now offers to put it back instead of asking you to buy another. In the Command Deck, **Settings > Anywhere** offers **Put it on this server** when your account has one free.
- In some cases, opening your Command Deck at your own domain showed a blank screen or a sign-in error. The sign-in page now loads and tells you what to do next.

## Storage

- **Accurate sizes before you import a pool.** In some cases, a pool that wasn't imported yet showed more usable space than it had once imported. HexOS now reads the pool's actual layout and shows **About** the size you'll get. When it can't tell yet, the card says **Size shown after import**.
- After you import a pool from the Storage page, its card now shows up right away, without a page reload.

## Folders

- When you keep your own recovery key, the encryption step of **New folder** now says that only your recovery key opens HexOS's copy of the folder's key, and reminds you to keep your emergency kit safe.
- When you edit an encrypted folder, the **Review** step now shows the folder's real key type.

## Buddy Backups

- **Restoring folders inside folders.** In some cases, a restored folder didn't come back, or a folder inside it came back read-only. Restored folders and the folders inside them now come back and are writable.
- If a folder inside your backup has its own passphrase, the restore now asks for it instead of failing.
- If you type the wrong passphrase, the **Unlock** dialog now stays open and tells you, so you can try again.
- A restore now shows its progress as soon as it starts.
- If you remove every folder from a backup and add one back, the backup now keeps its schedule.
- In some cases, the first Buddy Backup between two of your own servers failed until you tried again. It now works the first time.

## Virtual machines

- **Steadier Linux VMs.** In some cases, Linux VMs froze while showing console output or while shutting down because of their display driver. New Linux VMs made from HexOS templates now avoid that driver.

## Bring over

- In some cases, Bring over from a source reached through a VPN or a Tailscale subnet router stopped at the **Access** step with **That username or password didn't work**, even with the right password. It now connects.

## Help and security

- The **Community forum** link in the **Need help?** dialog now opens the [HexOS Hub](https://hub.hexos.com).
- We tightened security checks across folders, VMs, install scripts, sign-in and HexOS Anywhere.

## New curated apps

- [AdGuard Home](https://adguard.com/adguard-home/overview.html) - Block ads and trackers for every device in your home. **Pre-configure AdGuard Home** finishes its setup with the sign-in you choose at install.
- [Pi-hole](https://pi-hole.net) - Block ads and trackers across your whole network. **Pre-configure Pi-hole** sets the password you choose at install. You can run Pi-hole or AdGuard Home, but not both at once.
- [Stardew Valley](https://www.stardewvalley.net) - Farm together with friends on your own Stardew Valley server. HexOS runs the game server and its Steam sign-in as one app, and the Steam sign-in is never exposed on your network.
- [Rust](https://rust.facepunch.com) - Run your own Rust server. Its card shows who's online, and you can send console commands right from the card.

## App updates

- **More office editors.** Collabora now works with OpenCloud. Nextcloud can now connect to Euro-Office and ONLYOFFICE, as well as Collabora. Find them on each app's **Connections** tab.
- In some cases, when Nextcloud had a certificate set in TrueNAS, its setup steps and office connections never finished. They now work with a Nextcloud that only serves https.
- In some cases, the Jellyfin card kept asking you to sign in to see what's playing, even after you signed in. It now shows what's playing.

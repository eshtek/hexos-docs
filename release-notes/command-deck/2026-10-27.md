# October 27, 2026 - Bring over for everyone, UPS monitoring, six new apps, and more

Here is a summary of changes shipped since our [last update](/release-notes/command-deck/2026-10-16).

## Bring over

- **Bring over is now available to everyone.** Copy your files onto your server from another computer on your network, an external drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud Drive or iCloud Photos, with no sign-up and no beta badge. Google Drive support is still in review with Google and will follow.
- **See how far along each copy is.** In the activity tray, each share being copied now shows a progress bar and how much has been copied out of the total, the same way Buddy Backups transfers do.
- **Pause without losing your place.** Pause a copy and resume it later, and its progress picks up where it left off instead of starting again at zero.

## Power

- **Know when your UPS needs attention.** If your server is plugged into a UPS over USB, a new **Power** tile appears on your dashboard with the battery charge, time on battery, input voltage and load. It turns amber when the UPS is on battery or its battery is weak, and red when the battery is low or needs replacing.
- **UPS monitoring turns on by itself.** HexOS detects a USB UPS that was never set up and turns on monitoring, so TrueNAS can alert you about it. Your server does not shut down on its own until you choose to.
- **Power settings.** **Settings** > **Power** shows your UPS model and its latest reading. Choose whether the tile shows on your dashboard, and turn on **Shut down server when battery runs low**. A UPS you already set up in TrueNAS, including a network UPS, shows here too.

## A new welcome

- **Welcome to HexOS!** A new welcome opens on your server's dashboard. It recommends turning on **Hardware advisor** and offers to **Check your memory** before you start, then ends with shortcuts to bring over files, add folders, set up backups, explore the app catalog, configure users and install virtual machines.
- Every server sees the new welcome once. Choose **Don't show again** to close it for good, or open it again any time from **Settings** > **First-time setup**.

## Setup

- **A shorter setup.** The hardware questions moved out of setup and into the new welcome, so setup goes straight from your storage choices to the review.
- **Clearer storage choices.** The options for new storage pools are now called **Let HexOS decide**, **I'll configure myself** and **Use imported pools only**.

## Storage

- **Unlock a locked pool from Command Deck.** A pool protected by a passphrase locks again every time your server restarts. Its pool card now says **Locked**, and its pool page lets you unlock it with your passphrase. When the locked pool holds your apps, your dashboard says so and offers **Unlock** first, so your apps come back as soon as the pool opens.
- **Safer drive tests.** Tests that erase a drive, like **Full** and **Burn-in**, are now offered only for unused hard drives. When a drive belongs to a pool, its report explains why those tests are unavailable.
- **Fairer audits for drives that can't self-test.** Some NVMe drives cannot run a self-test. When their old error counts have not grown, a pool audit now marks them as ones to keep an eye on instead of failing them.
- In some cases, moving your apps to another pool could report a failure even though the move worked, and a second move soon after could leave your apps stopped. Moving your apps now reports its progress correctly and works when you move them again.

## Folders

- In some cases, encrypted folders created on a TrueNAS 26.0 beta could not be opened after their pool was moved or imported. HexOS now records their keys correctly and opens them.

## Apps

- **More dependable updates.** When you update several apps at once, the updates on each server now run one after another, so they no longer get in each other's way.
- **Patient downloads.** When Docker Hub limits how many downloads your server can make, HexOS now keeps retrying for about an hour instead of giving up after a minute or two.
- **Apps that need a display.** An app like Kodi that needs a graphics card is no longer offered for install on a server without one, and its page explains why. When you skip an app's optional setup, like Jellyfin's, HexOS tells you where to run it later.
- In some cases, an app update could show as failed while the app was still starting its new version. HexOS now waits for the app to finish starting before it decides.
- In some cases, installing or updating an app with its own database, like Jellystat, could change the database's file permissions so the app could not start. HexOS now leaves existing database files as they are.
- In some cases, an app could use an older copy of its setup script, so an option like PhotoStructure's HEIC and video support was skipped. Apps now always use the latest version.

## Virtual Machines

- **Plex stays up to date.** The Plex Media Server VM now installs the newest Plex and keeps it updated automatically, including Plex VMs you already have.
- **The right time in every VM.** Linux VMs from the catalog, like Fedora, Debian and Ubuntu, and the Plex Media Server VM now use your server's time zone. Custom Linux VMs start with the correct time.
- **More Windows 11 installers work.** Older Windows 11 installers, like 23H2, now install without stopping at a product key error.
- **Open the console from anywhere you connect.** A VM's console now opens when you reach your server through your own domain, and every click uses a fresh sign-in, so you no longer see **Not Authenticated**.
- **A calmer setup dialog.** **Set up a VM** no longer shows errors on fields you have not filled in yet, so links like **Open the official ISO download page** are visible from the start.
- **Cancel setup** now works for an install that was running when HexOS restarted.
- If a graphics card does not come back to your server after a VM releases it, **Settings** now says a restart is needed instead of listing the card as available, and apps waiting for it hold off until it is back.

## Buddy Backups

- **Know why a server is unavailable.** When you change the server that stores a backup, every other server on your account is listed, and any that cannot take the backup says why: it is the server being backed up, it already stores a backup from that server, or it is offline.
- **Backups on smaller screens.** The backups tables now fit on phones and narrow laptop screens, showing more columns as there is room.
- When your buddy's server is offline, a failed backup now says HexOS could not reach it and what to do, instead of an unrecognized reason.

## Design and reliability

- **A faster dashboard.** Your dashboard now loads noticeably faster, especially on servers with slower processors.
- **Your language stays put.** Opening an app, a folder or a details pane from your dashboard now keeps the language you chose. The language menus also note that languages other than English are provided by the community and may be incomplete.
- **Sign out everywhere on password change.** Changing your password now signs you out of every other session.
- **Important details in plain sight.** Details you need before confirming **Repair access**, accepting a backup request or uninstalling a VM now appear on the screen instead of inside tooltips.
- Menus opened from a details pane now always appear on top, even after you resize the window or rotate your phone.

## Coming soon to virtual machines

- HexOS can now install a NixOS server in one click. It will appear in the VM catalog soon.

## New curated apps

- [Kodi](https://kodi.tv) - Kodi plays your movies, shows and music on the TV plugged into your server. It is our first display app: it draws straight through your server's own graphics card, so no other computer or streaming box is needed.
- [Tautulli](https://tautulli.com) - Tautulli tracks what is watched on your Plex server. Its dashboard card shows what was recently added, who watches most, and when.
- [SABnzbd](https://sabnzbd.org) - SABnzbd is a Usenet downloader.
- [NZBGet](https://nzbget.com) - NZBGet is a lightweight Usenet downloader.
- [Deluge](https://deluge-torrent.org) - Deluge is a lightweight BitTorrent client.
- [Profilarr](https://github.com/Dictionarry-Hub/profilarr) - Profilarr manages quality profiles for Radarr and Sonarr.

## Media apps that work together

- **Connect your apps in one click.** Media apps can now link up with each other:
  - Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr connect to your download client: qBittorrent, Deluge, SABnzbd or NZBGet.
  - Prowlarr connects to Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr, and to each download client, so a search in Prowlarr itself can send what it finds.
  - Seerr, Bazarr and Profilarr connect to Radarr and Sonarr.
  - Jellyfin, Emby and Plex connect to Seerr, so you sign in to Seerr with your media server account.
  - Jellyfin connects to Jellystat, and Plex connects to Tautulli.

  HexOS offers these connections when you install an app beside another one, and from an installed app's **Connections** tab.
- **One admin account, chosen by you.** Media apps with their own sign-in no longer start with a default username and password. When you install them, you choose one admin username, email and password, and HexOS sets it up in each app. On your home network, most of these apps let you in without signing in.
- **Apps that open ready to use.** Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr start with your Movies, Shows and Music folders already added. Each download client keeps its downloads in its own folder, with downloads in progress kept apart from finished ones.
- **Set up your media server when you are ready.** Setting up Jellyfin and Emby during install is now optional. Run **Pre-configure Jellyfin**, **Pre-configure Emby** or **Pre-configure Plex** from the app's card any time later. **Pre-configure Plex** keeps the name of a server you already claimed.

## Richer app cards

- **Emby** now shows what is playing on its dashboard card.
- **Scrutiny** adds a slide of disk temperatures to its dashboard card.

### Curation notes

- **Kodi**
  - Kodi needs a graphics card with a TV or monitor plugged into it. If your server has no graphics card it can draw through, Kodi's page explains why it cannot be installed.
  - Your Movies, Shows and Music folders are in Kodi's library from the first start, and your Photos are ready to browse.
  - Kodi starts again on its own after your server restarts.
- **Tautulli**
  - When you install Plex, HexOS offers to install Tautulli beside it. If you already have both, use **Connect to Tautulli** on Plex's **Connections** tab to start tracking what is watched.
- **Prowlarr, SABnzbd and NZBGet**
  - HexOS sets up everything except your own accounts. Add your indexers in Prowlarr and your Usenet provider in SABnzbd or NZBGet.

**NOTE:** All of these updates are applied automatically to your Command Deck. You may need to clear your cache. Help with clearing your cache is available [here](/troubleshooting/browser-cache).

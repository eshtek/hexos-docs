# September 18, 2026 - A clearer walk through multi-step screens

Here is a summary of changes shipped since our [last update](/release-notes/command-deck/2026-09-17).

## Multi-step screens

- Creating a pool, expanding a pool, creating or editing a folder, installing a custom app, and burning an ISO to a USB drive now walk the same way: one named step at a time with a progress bar across the top, and **Continue** ready as soon as the step is complete.
- Every one of these flows ends on a review of your choices, laid out on cards. Each card now has an edit control that opens that step right where you are and brings you back to the review when you are done, so changing one answer at the end no longer means walking back through the whole screen.
- Screens that ask you to confirm something you cannot undo now state what will happen in one line you check off, in the same place every time.
- Dialogs now fit what is in them. A short form no longer stretches to fill a fixed height, and the multi-step screens have more room for the review cards.

## Mobile

- On a phone, a multi-step screen opens full screen with its heading and content at the top, while a single-step dialog slides up from the bottom. Dialogs opened from inside another dialog now open full screen as well, instead of stacking.

**NOTE:** All of these updates are applied automatically to your Command Deck. You may need to clear your cache. Help with clearing your cache is available [here](/troubleshooting/browser-cache).

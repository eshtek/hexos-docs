# October 27, 2026 - New app curations (Kodi, Tautulli, SABnzbd, NZBGet, Deluge & Profilarr)

Six new curated apps, media apps that connect to each other in one click, and richer app cards. Product changes from this update are in the [October 27 release note](/release-notes/command-deck/2026-10-27).

## New curated apps

- [Kodi](https://kodi.tv) - Kodi plays your movies, shows and music on the TV plugged into your server. It is our first display app: it draws straight through your server's own graphics card, so no other computer or streaming box is needed.
- [Tautulli](https://tautulli.com) - Tautulli tracks what is watched on your Plex server. Its dashboard card shows what was recently added, who watches most, and when.
- [SABnzbd](https://sabnzbd.org) - SABnzbd is a Usenet downloader.
- [NZBGet](https://nzbget.com) - NZBGet is a lightweight Usenet downloader.
- [Deluge](https://deluge-torrent.org) - Deluge is a lightweight BitTorrent client.
- [Profilarr](https://github.com/Dictionarry-Hub/profilarr) - Profilarr manages quality profiles for Radarr and Sonarr.

## Media apps that work together

- **Connect your apps in one click.** Media apps can now link up with each other:
  - Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr connect to your download client: qBittorrent, Deluge, SABnzbd or NZBGet.
  - Prowlarr connects to Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr, and to each download client, so a search in Prowlarr itself can send what it finds.
  - Seerr, Bazarr and Profilarr connect to Radarr and Sonarr.
  - Jellyfin, Emby and Plex connect to Seerr, so you sign in to Seerr with your media server account.
  - Jellyfin connects to Jellystat, and Plex connects to Tautulli.

  HexOS offers these connections when you install an app beside another one, and from an installed app's **Connections** tab.
- **One admin account, chosen by you.** Media apps with their own sign-in no longer start with a default username and password. When you install them, you choose one admin username, email and password, and HexOS sets it up in each app. On your home network, most of these apps let you in without signing in.
- **Apps that open ready to use.** Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr start with your Movies, Shows and Music folders already added. Each download client keeps its downloads in its own folder, with downloads in progress kept apart from finished ones.
- **Set up your media server when you are ready.** Setting up Jellyfin and Emby during install is now optional. Run **Pre-configure Jellyfin**, **Pre-configure Emby** or **Pre-configure Plex** from the app's card any time later. **Pre-configure Plex** keeps the name of a server you already claimed.

## Richer app cards

- **Emby** now shows what is playing on its dashboard card.
- **Scrutiny** adds a slide of disk temperatures to its dashboard card.

### Curation notes

- **Kodi**
  - Kodi needs a graphics card with a TV or monitor plugged into it. If your server has no graphics card it can draw through, Kodi's page explains why it cannot be installed.
  - Your Movies, Shows and Music folders are in Kodi's library from the first start, and your Photos are ready to browse.
  - Kodi starts again on its own after your server restarts.
- **Tautulli**
  - When you install Plex, HexOS offers to install Tautulli beside it. If you already have both, use **Connect to Tautulli** on Plex's **Connections** tab to start tracking what is watched.
- **Prowlarr, SABnzbd and NZBGet**
  - HexOS sets up everything except your own accounts. Add your indexers in Prowlarr and your Usenet provider in SABnzbd or NZBGet.

**NOTE:** All of these updates are applied automatically to your Command Deck. You may need to clear your cache. Help with clearing your cache is available [here](/troubleshooting/browser-cache).

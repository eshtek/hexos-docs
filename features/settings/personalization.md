# Personalization

Personalization sets how the Command Deck looks to you, and how it writes temperatures and dates. Your choices belong to your HexOS account, so they follow you on every server and every device you sign in from.

## Open the personalization screen

Click **Settings**, then click **Preferences**, then click **Personalization**.

<details>
<summary> The Preferences screen </summary>

![preferences-screen.png](/personalization/preferences-screen.png){.medium .framed}
</details>

## Choose how dates are written

1. Click the **Date format** menu, under **Temperature unit**.
2. Click **Month first (12/31/2026)** or **Day first (31.12.2026)**.

<details>
<summary> The Date format menu </summary>

![date-format-menu.png](/personalization/date-format-menu.png){.medium .framed}
</details>

Every date in the Command Deck changes at once: in sentences, in lists and next to times.

| Date format | A date on its own | A date in a sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **Month first (12/31/2026)** | 12/31/2026 | Dec 31, 2026 |
| **Day first (31.12.2026)** | 31.12.2026 | 31 Dec 2026 |

<details>
<summary> A date written day first </summary>

![date-day-first.png](/personalization/date-day-first.png){.medium .framed}
</details>

> **Info:** Month first is the default. Times keep the form they always had, for example 3:04 PM. While your server cannot reach the internet, its local Command Deck writes dates month first until it reconnects.
{.is-info}

## Choose the temperature unit

Click the **Temperature unit** menu and choose **Celsius (°C)** or **Fahrenheit (°F)**. Drive and processor temperatures change at once.
